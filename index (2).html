Privacy Policy

ONE OFF — The Stone Dash
Last updated: October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ONE OFF — The Stone Dash (the “Game”) handles information when you use the Android app.

Game information stored on your device

The Game saves game settings and high scores locally on your device so that your progress can persist between sessions. The Game does not require an account to play. Local game information may be lost if you clear the app's storage or uninstall it.

Advertising and third-party services

The Game uses Google AdMob to show banner advertisements and optional rewarded advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may collect or process data, such as advertising identifiers, IP addresses, device information, interactions with advertisements, and advertising performance information. This information may be used to serve, measure, and protect advertisements, subject to applicable permissions, consent requirements, and Google’s policies.

For details, see Google’s Privacy Policy and AdMob policies.

Rewarded advertisements

Rewarded ads are optional. If you choose to watch one, the advertising service determines whether the reward has been earned so the Game can provide the associated gameplay continuation.

Sharing of information

The Game does not require you to provide personal details to create an account. Advertising-related data may be processed and shared by Google and its advertising partners in accordance with their policies. The Game's locally saved high scores are not intentionally transmitted to the developer through a game account or online leaderboard.

Your choices and data retention

You can delete locally stored game data using Android’s app storage settings or by uninstalling the Game. Google manages advertising-related data according to its own policies and retention practices. Advertising privacy settings may also be available in your device settings.

Children and consent

The Game contains third-party advertising. Where required, consent mechanisms and age-appropriate advertising settings must be applied according to applicable laws, Google Play policies, and the Game's intended audience.

Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Game and its services change. Updates will be posted here, with the revision date updated above.

Contact

For privacy questions, please contact the developer using the developer contact information shown on the Game's Google Play listing.
